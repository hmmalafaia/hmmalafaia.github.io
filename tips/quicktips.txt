To add a blank margin around your QR code, use the -m flag followed by a number.
qrencode -m 10 -o qr_with_margin.png "Your text here"

If you are trying to keep a specific pixel size, remember that the margin is multiplied by your module size (-s).
For example, if you use -s 10 (each dot is 10 pixels) and -m 5 (a margin of 5 dots):
Your margin will be 50 pixels thick on all four sides.
Here is an example combining both:

qrencode -s 10 -m 5 -o my_qr.png "https://example.com"
